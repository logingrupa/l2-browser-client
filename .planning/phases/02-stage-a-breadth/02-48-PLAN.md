Plan index rule 14 puts this runtime wiring in 02-48, before the world lane's residency ring (02-49).

## Flagged assumptions

- Deviation from the plan index: the index puts `addNavigationCell` and `removeNavigationCell` on `meshNavigation.ts`. They live on the cell store in `navigationCells.ts`, which `createWorldNavigation` reads through `options.cells`, because the sink must exist before the world source resolves while the navigation is built after it (from the section's spawn candidate). The names are unchanged.
- Deviation from the plan index: a third sink method `requireNavigationCell(cellId, footprint)` is added. "Not ready while cells are missing" needs the loader to declare expected cells before their collision loads; without it a never-loaded neighbour is indistinguishable from open ground.
- `addNavigationCell` takes `NavigationCellCollision {terrainMeshes, obstacleMeshes}` instead of a flat mesh list, because terrain and obstacles follow different support rules today (two-sided terrain support versus upward-only obstacle support and sweeps).
- The scene-scoped context carries the sink and a player focus reader, because `loadNativeSection(scene, signal)` is world-owned, plan index rule 14 forbids lane plans from editing `src/runtime/**`, and streaming centred on the camera would drop collision under the player whenever the camera is framed elsewhere.
- Edge probe: WORLD-04 is unclassified (unresolved). The adjacency rule for shared footprint edges and the sorted `missingCellIds` are authored as truths anyway.
- Threat ids use the per-plan form `T-02-48-NN`, because the Phase 2 plans are written in parallel and no shared counter exists.
