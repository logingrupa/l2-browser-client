`scripts/actors/actor_census.py`:
- `census_actor_packages(client_access, lock_files, relative_paths)`: for each relative path (sorted), `require_equal_file(path)`, then `inspect_header(path)`. Row: `{path, packageVersion, licensee, wrapper, skeletalReaderGate, textureReaderGate}`. Gates are `"accepted" | "refused" | "not-applicable"`: `.ukx` and `.usk` get `skeletalReaderGate` from `skeletal_reader_gate(summary)` and `textureReaderGate: "not-applicable"`; `.utx` gets `textureReaderGate` from membership of `(version, licensee)` in `texture_mips.REVIEWED_VERSIONS` and `skeletalReaderGate: "not-applicable"`. An `unsupported-native` header becomes a row with `packageVersion: null`, `licensee: null` and both applicable gates `"refused"`, and its units become `decode-failed:<error>` gaps.
- `skeletal_reader_gate(summary)`: evaluates the reviewed pair by constructing `SkeletalReader` over a header-only package view (class `PackageHeaderView` with the header `summary` and a `properties` method returning an empty property map and no reader) with item `{"className": "SkeletalMesh"}`; a `FormatError` means `"refused"`. This keeps the reviewed pair owned by `skeletal.py` (no copied version list; chosen here, logged).
