## Decisions the orchestrator must revisit

1. **D-01, "the helper lock_file_identity or one like it".** `lock_file_identity` of the frozen guard returns only `(st_size, st_mtime_ns)` and raises the missing-lock refusal for a missing file, so it cannot serve. Measured here (Python 3.12.6, NTFS): the modification time moves in clock ticks of about 0.5 to 1 ms; 20 of 300 recreated files and 108 of 300 same-size rewrites in place kept their `st_mtime_ns`; `st_ino` changed in 300 of 300 recreations (it carries the NTFS sequence number). The plan adds `file_identity` with device and inode. The lock cache of D-02 keeps the guard's weaker identity, as it does today.
2. **D-01, the key.** `unit_directory_name` maps `x:a:b` and `x:a__b` to one folder, and today's check refuses the second key with the mismatch error. The plan keys by the step directory and the unit key.
3. **D-01, "begun and sealed again with the same fingerprint".** `begin` refuses a unit sealed with that fingerprint. The same-fingerprint test removes the step directory first, as the present error text tells a person to do.
4. **D-03, the profile.** The share rule is applied to a read-only profile of `inspected_tile_seals` over the two tiles this checkout holds fully sealed (15_20 and 18_20), not over the 30 tiles of drive E:. Measured per call at plan time: census read and validation 4.9 ms, `read_census_rows` 1.5 ms, `terrain_seam_rules_sha256` 1.5 ms, the path scan of `environment_lock_input_records` about 7.5 ms once the lock is kept. The expected result is that no reader reaches the share.
5. **D-04, what the cost ranks.** By `eBytes` the first 30 eligible tiles start 16_12, 18_10, 17_21, 17_20, 17_22, 17_25: the three measured heavy tiles are in the first six, so six workers start all three at once, but the map size does not rank them in their measured order. Phase A (raw) is cost-ordered too, through the same `outcomes`.
6. **D-01, cost that stays.** `sealed_receipt` still resolves the step directory through the frozen `confined_child_path` on every call: about 2 ms each, about 9 s of the 170 s.
