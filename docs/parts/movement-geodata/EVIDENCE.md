Cell-aware collision world (02-48, WORLD-04 and MOVE-01 mechanism, unit level):

- The MOVE-01 collision world (`createCollisionWorld` in `src/scene/world/movement/core/collisionWorld.ts`) holds decoded collision per streaming cell. `requireCell(cellId, footprint)` declares a cell the loader expects, `addCell(cellId, cell)` makes it resident, `removeCell(cellId)` drops its primitives and its requirement in one revision, and `collisionReadiness(position)` answers `ready` only inside a resident cell footprint, `cell-not-resident` inside a required cell not yet resident (it wins on a shared edge) and `no-collision-cell` elsewhere (e5b7326). A footprint edge shared by two cells belongs to both. `inspection()` returns `{requiredCellCount, residentCellCount, missingCellIds, revision}` with `missingCellIds` sorted.
- Cells come only from the 02-30 native-tile-collision resources: the per-chunk `VOLS`, `SMSH`, `PLAC`, `MOVR` and `TERR` groups and the per-map `BSPT` group, read through `src/shared/typedPayload.ts` and decoded by `decodeNativeTileCollisionCell` and `decodeNativeTileBspTree`. Every count, index and code is guarded with a `CollisionWorldError` that names the cell or tile, the group and the field; malformed BSPT leaf hull runs, volume scale or polygons and a non-positive TerrainScale fail the load, not the frame loop (02-REVIEW-FIX WR-50, with the read-only decode of the integration lane store). No mesh, visual LOD level or visibility flag reaches the collision world.
- Footprints, positions and every decoded primitive stay in float32 source units on the UE2 axes inside the core.
- The per-map BSP tree is fetched once per tile, shared by every chunk cell of that tile and released with the tile's last resident cell.
- `loadCollisionCell` in `src/scene/world/movement/loadCollisionCells.ts` fetches the chunk and tile `asset.json` by their SHA-256 pins (and the tile byte count), then `payload.bin` by the payload pin inside `asset.json`, refuses units other than `source`, axes other than `ue2-z-up` and any path outside `/l2/`, and adds the cell to the world with `addCell` in the same tick as the tile tree.
- `provideCollisionCellContext(scene, context)` and `collisionCellContextFor(scene)` hand the world and the player focus in metres to the world loader through a scene-keyed WeakMap (no conversion of that focus to source units); a second provider is refused and the context withdraws on scene dispose.
- `src/scene/world/movement/core/**` imports nothing outside itself except `src/shared/**`, no package, and type-checks against the ES2022 library alone, so no DOM global is reachable.
- No recast, navmesh, Babylon collisions, physics plugin or rapier in `src/scene/world/movement` (DEC-OWN-12 D7): the traces are our own port.

Collision traces and MoveActor (02-65, MOVE-01 core over WORLD-08 collision, unit level):

- `moveActor(world, actor, delta, rules, surroundings)` follows ULevel::MoveActor (wave 12 below): a float32 delta that `FVector::IsNearlyZero` accepts moves without a sweep; otherwise the actor box `(R, R, H)` sweeps the float32 TestDelta = Delta + 2 * DeltaDir, and a hit below time 1 moves by Time * TestDelta - 2 * DeltaDir with time (Distance - 2) / DeltaSize, or not at all with time 0 when Distance is at most 2. The hit carries the primitive (BSP tile, volume, placement, mover, terrain quad or pawn) and its declared source (`bsp-collision-tree`, `blocking-volume`, `static-mesh-slots`, the MOVR `static-slot-enable-collision`, `terrain-heightmap`, `pawn-cylinder`).
- `singleLineCheck(world, query, rules)` rounds the query to float32 and sweeps it without the extension; each primitive backs off as its client routine does (`hitTimeAfterBackoff`: BSP ray and terrain FClamp(f32(T - 0.5 / |D|), 0, 1), BSP box FClamp(f32(T - FClamp(0.1f, f32(0.1f / L), f32(4 / L))), 0, 1), static mesh the same with 1 / L, pawn 0), the smallest backed-off time wins and ties go to the primitive listed first (tile trees, then per cell volumes, placements, movers, terrain, then pawns).
- BSP trees (BSPT) and brush volumes (VOLS, in world space through the UE2 actor transform, axes from the shared `rotatorAxes` in `src/shared/math/rotator.ts`, 9c1fa7b) share one tree walk that carries the bRootOutside outside bit. Rays walk float32 segment points as the UModel line-check helper does and enter the first solid region; box sweeps collect the hull of the node just left (LeafHulls with the 0x40000000 flip bit and the hull box for BSPT, the path planes and the brush box for volumes) and clip against hull, box and bevel planes. The plane-limit and plane-node-range refusals stay at trace time in `readLeafHull`.
- Static mesh placements and movers clip triangles against the triangle bounds, both face planes and the edge planes; rays take the double-sided plane hit. Movers collide at the closed matrix unless `moverStates` marks them open. A sweep skips a placement or mover whose cached world bounds the swept box misses (one bounds test per resident placement, no spatial index; golden traces unchanged). Terrain runs in heightmap space from Location and TerrainScale (the converter's CalcCoords), with lifted triangles, the EdgeTurnBitmap split and QuadVisibilityBitmap 0 dropping the quad (our rule, row `collision.terrainHoles`); `traceTerrain` visits only the quad window a sweep can reach, bit-identical to the full chunk scan (02-REVIEW-FIX WR-51, WR-52).
- `pointCheck(world, query, rules)` goes front only when `d > 0` (a point on a plane is back) and stays separate from the visibility zone locate; box point checks walk hulls with the 1.1 push-out. `volumesContaining(world, point, rules)` returns the PhysicsVolumes around a point for the camera environment (02-64) and swimming (02-66).
- Every numeric rule is a row of `recipes/world/movement-native-rules.json` (`{id, value, provenance, status, interludeEvidence, runtimeDefault}`); `movement.maxStepHeight` and `movement.minFloorZ` live there for 02-66. The 14 rows of wave 12 are `confirmed-interlude` or `corrected-interlude` with Engine.dll or Core.dll evidence; the rest stay unconfirmed, ue2-data or unknown until POL-04 re-derives them.
- Golden traces: `tests/fixtures/movement/move-actor-bsp-wall.golden.json` (five declared moves against a synthetic BSP wall, for example a 300-unit move stopping at x = 241 = 256 - 9 - 4 - 2) and `tests/fixtures/movement/line-check-mixed-primitives.golden.json` (seven declared line checks) compare at 0 float32 ulp; `MOVEMENT_GOLDEN_WRITE=1` rewrites them. All fixtures are synthetic (D3).

Clean-room rewrite (wave 12, 2026-11-10, rows 704 and 705): the ray walk, box side filters, line-check backoff and MoveActor conversion follow the lock-equal Engine.dll `4dd2f63c...` and Core.dll `9462f87a...`, no longer the reference:

An inside leaf hits at the segment start with splitter node 0 at first, so a start in solid hits at time 0 with the root plane. LineCheck then takes Time = f32(f32(H . D) / f32(D . D)) and turns the normal against the motion when N . -D < 0 (0x107495AF-0x10749631). `ULevel::MultiLineCheck` 0x105C5E55 passes ExtraNodeFlags 0, so the start-in-solid skip for flag 0x10 never applies. Outside bits follow `FBspNode::ChildOutside` 0x10685750.
- Box side filters: the PointCheck walk 0x10746F00 visits front when Dist > -PushOut and back when Dist <= PushOut; the box LineCheck walk 0x10748120 visits front when either end is at least -PushOut and back when either is at most PushOut, front first when the end distance is not above the start distance. PushOut is f32(|f32(N.X * f32(E.X * 1.1f))| + ...) in both; point tests go front when the float32 distance is above 0.
- Backoff (`lineCheck.ts`, `movementRules.ts`): applied inside each primitive's LineCheck, not in ULevel (BSP ray, BSP box with Time preset 2.0, static mesh, terrain; addresses in the `collision.backoff.*` rows), and none in `UPrimitive::LineCheck` 0x10645DA0 beyond its own 0.001f cylinder offset.
- MoveActor conversion (`moveActor.ts`): `ULevel::MoveActor` 0x105C08A0 (rows `collision.moveActorExtension`, `collision.nearZeroDelta`: IsNearlyZero, each component strictly below 0.0001).

Behaviour changes, pinned in `tests/unit/movementLineCheck.test.ts` and `moveActor.test.ts`: a ray starting in solid reports the root splitter normal turned against the motion; the backoff, entry time, extended sweep and conversion are float32 stores (both golden traces regenerated); the recipe holds the client's float constants (0.1f, 0.001f, 1.1f widened).

- The movement presenter (`createMovementPresenter({collisionWorld, rules, reportIntent, pawnTraits})` in `src/scene/world/movement/movementPresenter.ts`) applies the 02-58 `move`, `stop`, `correct` and `teleport` events per entity and forgets a despawned one.
