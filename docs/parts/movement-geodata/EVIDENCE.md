Cell-aware collision world (02-48, WORLD-04 and MOVE-01 mechanism, unit level):

- The MOVE-01 collision world (`createCollisionWorld` in `src/scene/world/movement/core/collisionWorld.ts`) holds decoded collision per streaming cell. `requireCell(cellId, footprint)` declares a cell the loader expects, `addCell(cellId, cell)` makes it resident, `removeCell(cellId)` drops its primitives and its requirement in one revision, and `isReady(position)` is false at any point inside the closed footprint of a required cell that is not resident. A footprint edge shared by two cells belongs to both. `inspection()` returns `{requiredCellCount, residentCellCount, missingCellIds, revision}` with `missingCellIds` sorted.
- Cells come only from the 02-30 native-tile-collision resources: the per-chunk `VOLS`, `SMSH`, `PLAC`, `MOVR` and `TERR` groups and the per-map `BSPT` group, read through `src/shared/typedPayload.ts` and decoded by `decodeNativeTileCollisionCell` and `decodeNativeTileBspTree`. Every count, index and code is guarded with a `CollisionWorldError` that names the cell or tile, the group and the field. No mesh, visual LOD level or visibility flag reaches the collision world.
- Footprints, positions and every decoded primitive stay in float32 source units on the UE2 axes inside the core. The player focus is converted from metres with `worldPositionToSource` in `src/world/sourceFrame.ts` at the binding (`collisionFocusSourcePosition`).
- The per-map BSP tree is fetched once per tile, shared by every chunk cell of that tile and released with the tile's last resident cell.
- `loadCollisionCell` in `src/scene/world/movement/loadCollisionCells.ts` fetches the chunk and tile `asset.json` by their SHA-256 pins (and the tile byte count), then `payload.bin` by the payload pin inside `asset.json`, refuses units other than `source`, axes other than `ue2-z-up` and any path outside `/l2/`, and adds the cell to the world with `addCell` in the same tick as the tile tree.
- `provideCollisionCellContext(scene, context)` and `collisionCellContextFor(scene)` hand the world and the player focus to the world loader through a scene-keyed WeakMap; a second provider is refused and the context withdraws on scene dispose.
- `src/scene/world/movement/core/**` imports nothing outside itself except `src/shared/**`, no package, and type-checks against the ES2022 library alone, so no DOM global is reachable.
- No recast, navmesh, Babylon collisions or physics plugin in `src/scene/world/movement` (DEC-OWN-12 D7).

Collision traces and MoveActor (02-65, MOVE-01 core over WORLD-08 collision, unit level):

- `moveActor(world, actor, delta, rules, surroundings)` follows ULevel::MoveActor (wave 12 below): a float32 delta that `FVector::IsNearlyZero` accepts moves without a sweep; otherwise the actor box `(R, R, H)` sweeps the float32 TestDelta = Delta + 2 * DeltaDir, and a hit below time 1 moves by Time * TestDelta - 2 * DeltaDir with time (Distance - 2) / DeltaSize, or not at all with time 0 when Distance is at most 2. The hit carries the primitive (BSP tile, volume, placement, mover, terrain quad or pawn) and its declared source (`bsp-collision-tree`, `blocking-volume`, `static-mesh-slots`, the MOVR `static-slot-enable-collision`, `terrain-heightmap`, `pawn-cylinder`).
- `singleLineCheck(world, query, rules)` rounds the query to float32 and sweeps it without the extension; each primitive backs off as its client routine does (`hitTimeAfterBackoff`: BSP ray and terrain FClamp(f32(T - 0.5 / |D|), 0, 1), BSP box FClamp(f32(T - FClamp(0.1f, f32(0.1f / L), f32(4 / L))), 0, 1), static mesh the same with 1 / L, pawn 0), the smallest backed-off time wins and ties go to the primitive listed first (tile trees, then per cell volumes, placements, movers, terrain, then pawns).
- BSP trees (BSPT) and brush volumes (VOLS, in world space through the UE2 actor transform) share one tree walk that carries the bRootOutside outside bit. Rays walk float32 segment points as the UModel line-check helper does and enter the first solid region; box sweeps collect the hull of the node just left (LeafHulls with the 0x40000000 flip bit and the hull box for BSPT, the path planes and the brush box for volumes) and clip against hull, box and bevel planes.
- Static mesh placements and movers clip triangles against the triangle bounds, both face planes and the edge planes; rays take the double-sided plane hit. Movers collide at the closed matrix unless `moverStates` marks them open. Terrain runs in heightmap space from Location and TerrainScale (the converter's CalcCoords), with lifted triangles, the EdgeTurnBitmap split and QuadVisibilityBitmap 0 dropping the quad (our rule, row `collision.terrainHoles`).
- `pointCheck(world, query, rules)` goes front only when `d > 0` (a point on a plane is back) and stays separate from the visibility zone locate; box point checks walk hulls with the 1.1 push-out. `volumesContaining(world, point, rules)` returns the PhysicsVolumes around a point for the camera environment (02-64) and swimming (02-66).
- Every numeric rule is a row of `recipes/world/movement-native-rules.json` (`{id, value, provenance, status, interludeEvidence, runtimeDefault}`); `movement.maxStepHeight` and `movement.minFloorZ` live there for 02-66. The 14 rows of wave 12 are `confirmed-interlude` or `corrected-interlude` with Engine.dll or Core.dll evidence; the rest stay unconfirmed, ue2-data or unknown until POL-04 re-derives them.
- Golden traces: `tests/fixtures/movement/move-actor-bsp-wall.golden.json` (five declared moves against a synthetic BSP wall, for example a 300-unit move stopping at x = 241 = 256 - 9 - 4 - 2) and `tests/fixtures/movement/line-check-mixed-primitives.golden.json` (seven declared line checks) compare at 0 float32 ulp; `MOVEMENT_GOLDEN_WRITE=1` rewrites them. All fixtures are synthetic (D3).
- No recast, navmesh, Babylon collisions, physics plugin or rapier: the traces are our own port.

Clean-room rewrite (wave 12, 2026-11-10). An independent review found the ray walk, the box side filter, the line-check backoff and the MoveActor conversion followed the reference. They now follow the lock-equal Engine.dll `4dd2f63c...` and Core.dll `9462f87a...` (copied through `scripts/reference_client_guard.py`, hashed against the lock, disassembled with capstone, copies deleted):

- Ray walk (`bspCollisionTrace.ts`): the UModel line-check helper 0x10745B90 that `UModel::LineCheck` 0x107491D0 calls for a zero extent. It walks float32 segment points, not a time interval: a node goes front when both float32 plane distances exceed -0.001f and back when both are below 0.001f; otherwise Middle = f32(Start + f32(f32(Start - End) * f32(Dist1 / (Dist2 - Dist1)))), the near side (front when Dist1 > 0) recurses on Start to Middle, and the far side continues on Middle to End with the node as the hit splitter. An inside leaf hits at the segment start with splitter node 0 at first, so a start in solid hits at time 0 with the root plane. LineCheck then takes Time = f32(f32(H . D) / f32(D . D)) and turns the normal against the motion when N . -D < 0 (0x107495AF-0x10749631). `ULevel::MultiLineCheck` 0x105C5E55 passes ExtraNodeFlags 0, so the start-in-solid skip for flag 0x10 never applies. Outside bits follow `FBspNode::ChildOutside` 0x10685750.
- Box side filters: the PointCheck walk 0x10746F00 visits front when Dist > -PushOut and back when Dist <= PushOut; the box LineCheck walk 0x10748120 visits front when either end is at least -PushOut and back when either is at most PushOut, front first when the end distance is not above the start distance. PushOut is f32(|f32(N.X * f32(E.X * 1.1f))| + ...) in both; point tests go front when the float32 distance is above 0.
- Backoff (`lineCheck.ts`, `movementRules.ts`): applied inside each primitive's LineCheck, not in ULevel: `UModel::LineCheck` 0x10749319 (ray) and 0x107496FC (box, Time preset 2.0), `UStaticMesh::LineCheck` 0x1070354E, `ATerrainInfo::LineCheckWithQuad` 0x10721843, and none in `UPrimitive::LineCheck` 0x10645DA0 beyond its own 0.001f cylinder offset.
- MoveActor conversion (`moveActor.ts`): `ULevel::MoveActor` 0x105C08A0, TestAdjust 2.0f at 0x105C0BF7, conversion 0x105C0DEB-0x105C0EE5; IsNearlyZero is Core 0x10115FB0 (each component strictly below 0.0001).

Behaviour changes, pinned in `tests/unit/movementLineCheck.test.ts` and `moveActor.test.ts`: a ray starting in solid now reports the root splitter normal turned against the motion instead of a zero normal; the backoff, entry time, extended sweep and conversion are float32 stores, so `MOVEMENT_GOLDEN_WRITE=1` regenerated both golden traces (the wall moves differ by 1 ulp, the line checks by up to 4 ulp); the recipe now holds the client's float constants (0.1f, 0.001f and 1.1f widened to double).
