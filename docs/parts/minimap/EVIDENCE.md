# Minimap and world map evidence

Part `minimap`, lane `world`. What passes now, what is open, and where the proof lives. Keep this file under about 30 KB.

## Passes now

- Route recipe (02-97, WORLD-06 data side): `recipes/world/minimap-radar-route.json` (SHA-256 `7ef3dd50...`) pins interface.u, nwindow.u, Core.dll, engine.dll (lock class equal), nwindow.dll (class differing) and Localization.ini (class volatile-config) at their lock entries, and holds the verified route: NewRadarWnd picks the radar texture by the ShowZoneTitle zone name, 17 GetLocInfo cases matched whole-string and case-insensitive (`appStricmp` in `UObject::execSwitch`), else `RadarMaps.Global`; the world map is six `L2Font-e.Minimap.int_worldmap1` to `6` DXT1 pieces, 2 columns by 3 rows of 1024, with the global GetMinimapPosition projection; the zone key comes from the L2ZoneName sampler and helper 0x1044bf90. `minimap_radar_route_recipe.py` reads it strictly; the real-client test re-reads the exports, the case literals in the GetLocInfo bytecode, the format 5 town maps, the six pieces, one zone key texture per locked tile and the zonename key facts from the lock-equal E:/Lineage2-client.
- Route unit `minimap-route:interlude` (`scripts/world/minimap_route_step.py`): converts the 17 town maps, `RadarMaps.Global` and the six pieces once through the minimap texture Converter and seals one `native-minimap-route` v1 manifest (`maps/cde3726a.../native-minimap-route/manifest.json`, 120,535 bytes). All 24 archives are in the world lane store. Each of the 17 format 5 (RGBA8) maps equals the recipe's B,G,R,A decode of mip 0 pixel for pixel (`pixelCheck` status `equal`, 1024 x 1024). The command guards its client and holds the world lane lock.
- Tile minimap schema 2 (`scripts/world/minimap_step.py`, step version 2): each tile pins the route manifest by path and SHA-256, carries its zone key texture `l2zonename.<tile id>` (rule `zone-sampler-tile`), its hunting zones in the frame, `radarTexture` and `projection` null, and the route gaps plus its own. The unit config holds the raw, chunk plan, converter and route fingerprints; the inputs are the tile map, l2zonename.utx, zonename-e.dat and huntingzone-e.dat.
- 02-97 batch (one process, 145 tiles, exit 0, lane catalog `43a5132d...`): 146 tile minimaps are schema 2, every one with a zone key texture (214 hunting zones in all). Gaps: `minimap-join-unresolved:zone-title-source` 146, `minimap-join-unresolved:radar-anchor` 146, `minimap-join-unresolved:zone-key-run` 3 (20_14, 21_25, 25_19). No row, census or manifest holds a radar-texture, zone-grid or projection reason.
- Gap rows and coverage (same catalog): `gap-rows/minimap.json` holds 146 gap rows and 2 pending on an upstream failure (terrain, meshes). Each row names the first gap reason in sort order, `radar-anchor`; the product of every gapped tile also lists `zone-title-source`. The coverage part counts the 153 locked tiles: 0 converted, 146 gap, 7 pending.
- Census `docs/parts/minimap/minimap-join-census.json` (schema 2, `python -B scripts/world/minimap_join_census.py --client E:/Lineage2-client`, 27,556 bytes, SHA-256 `37a9baa2...`): the 17 cases with format RGBA8 and the zonename rows whose Name equals the case literal (16 cases 1 or 2 rows; `Town  of Goddard` with two spaces matches no row), the Global default (DXT1), the six DXT1 pieces, the zone keys (369 rows, 304 keys, 21 global rows below Color 200, 45 shared keys holding 110 rows, 3 overlapping runs), the MinimapCtrl controls `MinimapWnd.Minimap` and `NewRadarWnd.specialHiddenMap`, the route gaps once and one row per locked tile.
- Browser parser `src/scene/world/minimapJoin.ts`: parses schema 2 and refuses schema 1, a route pin whose path does not name its SHA-256 and a zone key texture rule other than `zone-sampler-tile`. With a null projection `minimapTexturePosition` returns the first gap reason.
- Tests: `tests/pipeline/test_minimap_radar_route_recipe.py` (12, the 6 client cases run), `test_minimap_route_step.py` (8), `test_minimap_step.py` (18), `test_minimap_join.py` (10), `test_zone_name_keys.py` (3) and `tests/unit/minimapJoin.test.ts` (7). `world-minimap.spec.ts` on the data-ui store checks the real route pins: the 20_21 route manifest and every archive it pins exist with their SHA-256.

It moves to the route in 02-89: NewRadarWnd.TexMap by zone name, the MinimapWnd world map from the six pieces and the projection, and the zone sampler for the zone name text boxes. After that the two null fields, the synthetic radar fixtures and the tile-grid arrangement are deleted.
